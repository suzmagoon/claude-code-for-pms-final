What happened

How a faster timeout and one scoring rule shut four responders out.

On 12 Aug, 4.2 cut the ping wait from 90 s to 60s and shifted ranking weight toward proximity. Missing a ping scores the same as refusing one, and the only way back up the list is taking a callout. A responder who goes quiet stops being asked, so they can't recover.

Module 5 · Super Speed

Build the fix nobody had

My prompt (05-super-speed/prompts.md, #16)I want the console to cleanly show my responders ranking at the top, and then the two responders as cards within which I can see their status (Active, Gone quiet, Recovering, and Away), if they've gone quiet, why, and a way to open a modal to manage their pings. The modal to manage their pings should give me a way to assign pings to them, and notify them, or set them to 'away' if they're unavailable for some reason.
Finding / artifact

A "way back in" for quiet responders:

  • First, log the ranking and derive a "gone quiet" state (no routing change)
  • Then, a smaller penalty for missed pings plus decay toward 0.5
  • Then, a re-entry ping for a quiet, fully capable responder near the top
  • Shipped as separate stages, because 4.2 changed two things at once

Keeping it from happening again

A failsafe: nothing proceeds until it clears six gates

On 14 Aug, Marcus asked whether the new weighting should apply to responders who'd been turning jobs down. Nobody owned the question, so nobody answered it. It has sat unanswered for 56 days while four responders went silent. The checklist is the gate every brief must clear before it goes further.

Wrap-up · what we found, concluded and propose

Release 4.2 in one page

What we found
  • Acceptance 76.6% → 64.0%; missed pings 2% → 18%. Not seasonal. (M1, M3)
  • Four responders fell 37 → 0 takes a week; the work moved to the other twelve. (M3)
  • Tickets jumped from ~7 to 20–32 a week, 83 still open. Interviews alone pointed at the wrong problems. (M2)
What we concluded
  • 4.2 changed the wait and the weighting at once; the shorter wait is the likelier culprit, but untested. (M4)
  • A missed ping costs the same as a refusal, and the only way back is taking a callout. Quiet becomes permanent. (M4)
  • Marcus's 14 Aug question is answered by the code: the weights apply to everyone. (M4)
What we propose
  • A way back in, in three stages: log and "gone quiet" state, softer miss penalty with decay, re-entry ping. (M5)
  • Test the 60s wait and the weighting separately.
  • Gate every brief with /review-checklist. (M6)
